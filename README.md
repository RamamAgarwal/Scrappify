# Scrappify
### One-Step Solution for Institutional E-Waste Management

> Making e-waste disposal simple, transparent, and sustainable for educational institutions.

---

## About

Scrappify is a web-based platform developed to revolutionize the way educational institutions manage electronic waste.

Schools, colleges, and universities often lack a standardized, transparent, and compliant system for disposing of obsolete electronic equipment. As a result, a significant portion of institutional e-waste is either improperly handled or disposed of through informal channels, contributing to environmental degradation.

Scrappify bridges this gap by connecting institutions with certified recycling partners through an easy-to-use digital platform. Institutions can schedule pickups, track waste collection, monitor recycling status, receive compliance documentation, and contribute towards a more sustainable circular economy.

Our vision is to create a reliable digital ecosystem where institutions can responsibly dispose of e-waste while promoting environmental sustainability.

---

# Problem Statement

Electronic waste is one of the fastest-growing waste streams globally.

Despite increasing awareness regarding sustainability, many educational institutions still struggle with:

- Lack of standardized e-waste disposal systems
- Dependence on informal waste collection networks
- Low awareness of certified recycling methods
- Compliance and documentation challenges
- Difficulty finding trusted recycling partners
- No digital platform for scheduling pickups or tracking disposal

These challenges often lead to improper disposal practices, environmental pollution, and missed opportunities for responsible recycling.

---

# Our Solution

Scrappify provides an end-to-end digital platform that simplifies institutional e-waste management.

The platform enables institutions to:

- Schedule e-waste pickups
- Connect with certified recycling partners
- Track the complete disposal lifecycle
- Ensure proper segregation of electronic waste
- Receive transparent valuation of recyclable materials
- Generate sustainability and compliance reports

Instead of relying on fragmented disposal systems, institutions can manage the complete recycling process from a single platform.
